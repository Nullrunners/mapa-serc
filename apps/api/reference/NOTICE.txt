census-2021-pl.csv.gz
=====================

Residents of Poland in the 1 km statistical grid (ETRS89-LAEA, EPSG:3035), census of 31.03.2021.

Source: Eurostat, Census 2021 population grid, version 3 (file ESTAT_Census_2021_V3.csv in
https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/census/2021/Eurostat_Census-GRID_2021_V3.zip).
Data for Poland delivered by Główny Urząd Statystyczny (Narodowy Spis Powszechny Ludności i Mieszkań 2021).

Modifications made by AED Hub (scripts/fetch-population.ts):
- kept only cells whose CNTR_ID includes PL and whose total population T is above 0 (197 439 cells,
  37 092 675 residents; 856 border cells also include residents reported by a neighbouring country);
- kept only the columns: n_km, e_km (south-west corner of the cell in kilometres, taken from GRD_ID),
  t (total population) and y_ge65 (population aged 65 and over);
- y_ge65 values withheld for confidentiality (-8888) or not available (-9999) are written as 0
  (56 807 cells), and values above t are cut to t. The number of seniors is therefore a lower bound.

The gap analysis (src/gaps) further divides each cell evenly among the H3 hexagons whose centre lies in it.
